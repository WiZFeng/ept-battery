1

Description of Hazardous Substances

This battery does not contain mercury, cadmium or lead exceeding the regulatory limits. The alkaline electrolyte (potassium hydroxide) contained is corrosive and may cause irritation if leaked. Do not short-circuit, disassemble, puncture or throw it into the fire.

Correct Classification and Placement Guidelines
