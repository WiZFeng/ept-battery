III. Waste Battery Management
1

Description of Hazardous Substances

This battery does not contain mercury, cadmium or lead exceeding the regulatory limits. The lithium-ion electrolyte contained may cause fire when damaged. Do not puncture or throw it into the fire.

The battery is collected separately
2

Correct Classification and Placement Guidelines

  1. Do not discard in household garbage or mixed garbage.
  2. Please place the used batteries in the designated battery recycling bins or return them to the retailer's recycling points.
IV. Warnings for Use
  1. Don't immerse the battery in water and seawater. Please put it in cool and dry environment if no using.
  2. Do not discard or leave the battery near a heat source as fire or heater.
  3. Being charged, using the battery charger specifically for that purpose.
  4. Don't reverse the positive and negative terminals.
  5. Don't connect the battery to an electrical outlet directly.
  6. Don't connect the positive and negative terminal directly with metal objects such as wire. Short terminals of battery is strictly prohibited, it may damage battery.
  7. Do not transport and store the battery together with metal objects such as necklaces, hairpins.
  8. Do not strike, throw or trample the battery.
  9. Do not directly solder the battery and pierce the battery with a nail or other sharp object.
  10. Do not use lithium ion battery and others different lithium polymer battery model in mixture.
  11. Prohibition of use of damaged cells.
  12. Don't bend or fold sealing edge. Don't open or deform folding edge. Don't fillet the end of the folding edge.
  13. Don't fall, hit, bend battery body.
